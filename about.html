Our Mission

Transparent pricing for every fan.

Hidden fees are the norm in the ticket industry. A $150 seat becomes $210 by the time you reach checkout — service fees, order fees, facility charges, delivery fees. TicketCompass shows you the all-in total upfront, across every platform, so you can compare on equal footing and buy with confidence.

We don't sell tickets. We don't take a cut from your order total. We simply show you where the best deal is.

What We Do

One search. Three platforms. The best price.

We pull pricing data from Ticketmaster, StubHub, and SeatGeek — the three largest ticket platforms — and display every fee so the number you see is the number you pay. No last-minute surprises.

Our Principles

How we stay honest.

⚖️
Platform-neutral
We compare every platform equally. We don't prioritize a partner's listing or hide a better deal because it's on a different site.
🔍
All-in only
Every price shown includes all fees. We never display a base price that inflates at checkout.
🚫
No ticket sales
We are a comparison tool, not a seller. Purchases happen entirely on the platform's own secure checkout.
📢
Clear disclosure
We earn a commission when you buy through our links. This is disclosed prominently and never affects what prices we show.
Independence

We answer to fans, not platforms.

TicketCompass is an independent company. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any professional sports league, team, or ticket platform. No platform pays for placement or ranking on our site.

Our revenue comes from standard affiliate commissions — a small percentage of the ticket price paid by the platform when a purchase is made through our link, at no additional cost to you.
