Privacy Policy & Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: August 2026

Affiliate Disclosure: TicketCompass participates in affiliate programs with ticket platforms including Ticketmaster, StubHub, and SeatGeek. When you click a link on this site and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. This does not influence which platforms we compare or the prices we display.

About TicketCompass

TicketCompass is an independent ticket price comparison service. We compare all-in prices across major ticket platforms — Ticketmaster, StubHub, and SeatGeek — so fans can find the best deal without hidden fees. We do not sell tickets directly. When you click a ticket listing, you are redirected to the platform's website to complete your purchase.

TicketCompass is operated independently and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any professional sports league, team, or ticket platform.

Information We Collect

TicketCompass does not require account creation, login, or any form of personal registration. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information.

Our site may automatically collect limited technical data as part of normal web server operation, including:

  • IP address (used to determine approximate region for analytics)
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Referring website

This data is collected in aggregate by our hosting provider (Cloudflare Pages) and is used solely to understand site traffic and performance. It is not sold or shared with third parties.

Local Storage

To improve loading speed, TicketCompass stores sports schedule data (game dates, teams, venues) in your browser's local storage with a one-hour expiration. This data contains no personal information — it is the same public schedule data visible to all visitors. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Cookies

TicketCompass itself does not set tracking cookies. However, third-party platforms linked from this site (Ticketmaster, StubHub, SeatGeek) may set their own cookies when you visit their websites. Those platforms operate under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

Affiliate Disclosure

TicketCompass participates in affiliate marketing programs. This means that when you click a link to Ticketmaster, StubHub, SeatGeek, or another ticket platform and complete a purchase, TicketCompass may receive a commission from that platform.

This commission comes from the platform, not from you — the price you pay is the same whether you arrive at their site through TicketCompass or directly. Affiliate relationships do not influence our price comparisons, rankings, or editorial content.

This disclosure is made in accordance with the Federal Trade Commission's guidelines on endorsements and testimonials (16 CFR Part 255).

Prices displayed on TicketCompass are illustrative and intended to reflect general market ranges. They are not guaranteed and may differ from the actual price shown at checkout on any platform. Always confirm the final price on the platform before purchasing. TicketCompass is not responsible for pricing discrepancies.

TicketCompass is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our site, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.
